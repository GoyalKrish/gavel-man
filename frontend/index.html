Methodology

Deterministic Instruction Counting vs. Wall-Clock Noise

Standard timing libraries like std::chrono measure elapsed wall-clock seconds, which fluctuate due to CPU frequency scaling, thread preemption, cache warmth, and thermal throttling. These factors distort curve fits and make sub-linear or linearithmic differences difficult to distinguish.

THE JUDGE avoids wall-clock noise by executing binaries inside the Valgrind Callgrind virtualized environment, counting the exact number of CPU instruction reads (Ir). With --collect-atstart=no --toggle-collect=main, runtime initialization and dynamic linker steps are bypassed, isolating instructions executed exclusively within main().

Data-Space Ordinary Least Squares (OLS)

The profiler fits measurement pairs (N, I(N)) to 9 candidate models of the form I(N) = c · f(N) + b directly in data space. Data-space regression preserves fixed additive overhead b without the distortion introduced by logarithmic transformations. Power-law log-log slopes are evaluated concurrently to extract the scaling exponent k, and candidates with non-physical negative coefficients (c < 0) are discarded.
